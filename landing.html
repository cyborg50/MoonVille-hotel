Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is the check-in / check-out time?

Check-in is available 24x7, as per your convenience. The standard check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability — please confirm at the time of booking.

Is the area safe for solo travelers?

Absolutely! Sector-116, Noida is a well-maintained, safe residential area. We have 24x7 CCTV surveillance, secure entry, and our front desk is always staffed. Solo travelers — especially women — feel completely safe staying with us.

Do you accept local ID for check-in?

Yes, we accept all valid government-issued photo IDs including Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License, and Passport. As per government guidelines, a valid photo ID is required at the time of check-in for all guests.

Is parking available?

Yes, we offer free parking for both two-wheelers and cars directly on our premises. No need to hunt for street parking — your vehicle is safe and secure during your stay.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes! We offer free cancellation up to 2 hours before your scheduled check-in. No questions asked. We believe in flexibility because plans change — and your comfort is our priority.

Do you provide room service?

Yes, complimentary room service is available for selected items. We also offer complimentary breakfast with all room bookings. Additional food and beverage options can be arranged upon request.

Is food available at the hotel?

Yes! Complimentary breakfast is included with all bookings. We also serve a selection of meals throughout the day. Plus, Sector-116 has plenty of local eateries and restaurants just around the corner.

How far is the nearest metro?

The nearest metro station is Sector 52 on the Noida Metro (Aqua Line), approximately 5 minutes by auto or cab. It connects you to Greater Noida, Noida City Centre, and beyond.
